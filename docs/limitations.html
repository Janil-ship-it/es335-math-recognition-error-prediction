Limitations

What we claim — and what we do not

Synthetic development data ES 335 · IITGN
In short. We claim method performance on this recogniser setup and this development proxy (and course scans when available). We do not claim transfer to other scanners or a statistically stable superiority with only six test groups.

What we claim

On this feature set and development proxy, a learned flagger can rank lines so that, at the same review budget, more true recognition errors are caught than by a fixed confidence threshold. All gains are reported with student-level bootstrap intervals.

What we do not claim

  • Transfer to other recognisers, scanners, subjects, or assignment formats.
  • That digitisation alone makes grading correct.
  • Statistically stable superiority: Δ intervals currently include zero.
  • Token or time savings unless measured with logged settings.

Known limitations

  • Small test set (six student groups) → wide confidence intervals.
  • Development proxy, not yet IITGN course scripts.
  • Line detector is a research prototype.
  • Real HMER weights optional; proxy path used for offline reproducibility.
